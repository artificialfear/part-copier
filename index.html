    Clean up a scanned set

    For PDFs made on a copier or phone. Straightens crooked pages, turns grey paper white, removes scanner shadows and blank pages, and fits 9×12 parts onto letter paper. Clean up before marking parts in the page map — removing blank pages renumbers them.

    ColorBlack & white is crispest and makes the smallest files; grayscale keeps faint pencil marks.
    DarknessRaise it if notes or staff lines come out broken; lower it if the paper looks speckled.
    Page sizeFit to letter trims the empty margins, then scales the music to fill an 8.5×11 page so nothing gets cut off.
    Quality300 dpi is sharpest; 200 dpi makes smaller files and works faster.

      4Print
