# Part Copier changelog

## 2026-11-01
- Percussion grid: choose which percussionists play which part, per piece and ensemble.
- "Check before printing" in step 4: missing chairs, split-point ties, old rosters, students with no
  instrument, percussionists with no part. Downloading needs a tick once you've checked.

## 2026-10-29
- ePrint sets: repeated copies of a part are recognized and skipped; the score is found from its left margin;
  mallet parts (Bells, Chimes…) stay separate.

## 2026-10-28
- Numbered flex-band sets (Part 1–5 in several keys) split by chair across voices, with a warning for uncovered voices.
- Flex parts without separators ("Clarinet in Bb Trumpet in Bb"); Keynote-style PDFs whose part names are artwork.
- Student names move to a blank spot instead of covering QR codes.
- Mellophone, sousaphone and marching percussion (snare, bass drum, tenors/quints, cymbals).

## 2026-10-27
- Phone-scan hidden text layers are removed from print files (copiers were stopping part-way).
- Part detection keeps 2-page parts together when a first page is unreadable.

## 2026-10-21
- Replace one part with a better PDF; reprint only some parts for lost music; collapsible steps.
