# Part Copier changelog

## 2026-11-02 (night)
- **Lost-part reprints from the Raven Band Hub:** the Hub's Music requests → "Reprint in Part Copier" opens the piece straight in
  "Reprint only some parts", with each student's part ticked and their name on it. Students not on a roster get a blank copy of
  their instrument's part; anything it can't match is listed so you can pick it.

## 2026-11-02 (evening)
- **Keep in sync through Google** (step 1 → Use this setup on another computer): your ensembles, counts, rosters, print rules
  and print history follow you to any computer where you sign in. Stored in Part Copier's own private folder in your Google
  Drive, which can't see your other files. If two computers both changed something, it asks which to keep.
- **Print history** in step 4: every downloaded print PDF (piece, ensemble, copies, reprints and who they were for), with
  "printed N times, last …" for the piece you're working on.
- Google sign-in asks for one more permission the first time (the private sync folder).

## 2026-11-02
- Pieces loaded from the Music Library bring their score along (it only prints if you click Print a copy).
- Score: one-click **Print a copy** on the Score row; the score can also be picked in "Reprint only some parts".

## 2026-11-02
- Load a piece straight from Google Drive: step 2 → "Load a piece from your Music Library" lists every piece with a
  PDF Folder link; pick one and its part PDFs load. The Raven Band Hub's library links here too.
- Google sign-in now also asks for read-only Drive access (one extra consent the first time).
- "Find my Music Library" searches your Drive for the library sheet, so no link to paste; the loader is always shown
  (with a note to connect Google in step 1 first).

## 2026-11-01 (evening)
- Pull the roster straight from a Google Sheet (read-only Google sign-in); auto-refresh on open while signed in;
  step 4 offers a pull when the roster is over 12 hours old.

## 2026-11-01 (later)
- Scans and ePrints read about 40% faster (two readers, repeated copies read once, time-left estimate).
- Hand-out list PDF: each part and its students in chair order.
- Flex sets: an empty voice is covered by moving the lowest player from a nearby voice.
- Print files keep visible text and drop only the invisible scanner layer (and fonts nothing uses).
- Test suite with made-up fixtures in tests/.

## 2026-11-01
- Percussion grid: choose which percussionists play which part, per piece and ensemble.
- "Check before printing" in step 4: missing chairs, split-point ties, old rosters, students with no
  instrument, percussionists with no part. Downloading needs a tick once you've checked.

## 2026-10-29
- ePrint sets: repeated copies of a part are recognized and skipped; the score is found from its left margin;
  mallet parts (Bells, Chimes…) stay separate.

## 2026-10-28
- Numbered flex-band sets (Part 1–5 in several keys) split by chair across voices, with a warning for uncovered voices.
- Flex parts without separators ("Clarinet in Bb Trumpet in Bb"); Keynote-style PDFs whose part names are artwork.
- Student names move to a blank spot instead of covering QR codes.
- Mellophone, sousaphone and marching percussion (snare, bass drum, tenors/quints, cymbals).

## 2026-10-27
- Phone-scan hidden text layers are removed from print files (copiers were stopping part-way).
- Part detection keeps 2-page parts together when a first page is unreadable.

## 2026-10-21
- Replace one part with a better PDF; reprint only some parts for lost music; collapsible steps.
